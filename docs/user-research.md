**Requirement:** 3–5 real discussions with potential users, or a substantive agreement with a real client. **No interviews have been claimed here.**

## Interview guide
1. Do you receive suspicious messages? What makes them hard to evaluate?
2. What do you normally do before opening a link or responding?
3. Would you paste the message into an app to assess risk? Why or why not?
4. What explanation of a warning would be understandable?
5. What privacy concerns would you have about sharing message text with a service?
6. Which languages do you most often receive messages in?
7. What should happen when the app is uncertain?
